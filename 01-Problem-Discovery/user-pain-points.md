# User Pain Points Analysis

This document details the critical pain points experienced by early-career candidates (final-year students and recent graduates) across the 6-stage job search decision lifecycle:

```
DISCOVER  ──▶  UNDERSTAND  ──▶  EVALUATE  ──▶  PRIORITIZE  ──▶  ACT  ──▶  TRACK
```

## Detailed Pain Point Breakdown by Journey Stage

### 1. Stage: DISCOVER
* **Core Friction**: Sorting through voluminous, uncurated listings with inconsistent title taxonomy and inflated experience expectations.
* **Key Risk**: Candidate wastes the first 30–45 minutes of every search session filtering out mismatched roles `[ASSUMPTION]`.

### 2. Stage: UNDERSTAND
* **Core Friction**: Decoding ambiguous job descriptions that blend responsibilities, company fluff, and exhaustive technical stacks.
* **Key Risk**: Candidate misinterprets role expectations and self-selects out of roles they could qualify for, or vice versa `[PRODUCT INFERENCE]`.

### 3. Stage: EVALUATE
* **Core Friction**: Evaluating multi-dimensional fit (technical stack, domain, project alignment, qualification baseline) without explainable tooling.
* **Key Risk**: Decisions are made based on superficial keyword overlap rather than substantive competency alignment `[PRODUCT INFERENCE]`.

### 4. Stage: PRIORITIZE
* **Core Friction**: Deciding which 5–10 opportunities to prioritize when 50+ opportunities appear viable on the surface.
* **Key Risk**: Candidate spreads effort thin across too many low-conviction applications instead of focusing on high-fit targets `[ASSUMPTION]`.

### 5. Stage: ACT
* **Core Friction**: Aligning candidate narrative, project highlights, and resume framing to specific role demands under time pressure.
* **Key Risk**: Reversion to mass-submitting generic materials `[PRODUCT INFERENCE]`.

### 6. Stage: TRACK
* **Core Friction**: Manual, disconnected data entry to log applied jobs, job description snapshots, and pipeline stages.
* **Key Risk**: JDs get deleted by employers after closing, leaving candidate with zero reference when contacted for screening `[FACT]`.
